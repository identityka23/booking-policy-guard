# Privacy Policy — Booking Policy Guard

**Last updated:** 5 October 2026
**Provider:** the individual developer of Booking Policy Guard, based in Türkiye
**Contact:** identityka@gmail.com

## 1. What the app does
Booking Policy Guard is a **read-only** app for Wix sites that use Wix Bookings. When you press "Sync bookings", it reads your own booking, policy and payment data from your Wix site and shows you what is measurable: how many bookings can be analysed, how often clients no-show or cancel, and which attended bookings have no payment recorded in Wix. It does not change your bookings, orders, policies or payments, and it does not charge or refund anyone.

## 2. Data the app reads from your Wix site
Only after you install the app and approve its permissions, and only on your own site:
- booking status, payment status, session date and time, and service ID;
- attendance status (attended or not attended);
- the booking policy that applied to each booking (cancellation and rescheduling rules, whether a cancellation fee is enabled);
- order totals, payments and refunds (amounts and statuses) for those bookings.

The permissions the app asks for are: *Read Bookings – Public Data*, *Read Bookings – Including Participants* and *Read Orders*. The app asks for no permission to change anything.

## 3. Data the app does not store
Although Wix may return customer details together with a booking, the app **does not copy or store** customer names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses, and it never reads payment card details. It also does not read data from your site visitors or from other apps.

## 4. What the app stores, and where
For each booking the app stores a small record in a private data collection **inside your own Wix site**: booking, service, policy and order identifiers, statuses, a fingerprint of the policy, and payment amounts (in the smallest currency unit). These identifiers are technical IDs that only have meaning inside your Wix site.

The app runs on Wix's own infrastructure. It does not send your data to any server operated by the developer or to any third party, and it does not use advertising, analytics or tracking technologies.

## 5. How the data is used
Only to show you the numbers and observations in the app's dashboard page. The data is not sold, shared or used to train models.

## 6. Retention and deletion
- The records stay in your site until you delete them.
- You can delete everything the app stored at any time: open the app, then **Support and data → Delete stored data**.
- **Before you uninstall the app, please press "Delete stored data".** We recommend this because automatic removal of the stored records at uninstall has not been confirmed.
- You can also ask us to help with a data question by writing to identityka@gmail.com.

## 7. Your responsibilities and legal basis
You decide whether to install the app. The data the app reads belongs to your business and your customers. If your customers' personal data is protected by law where you operate (for example GDPR or KVKK), you remain responsible for your own obligations towards them; the app is designed so that it does not store that personal data.

## 8. Security
The app can reach your site's data only through the permissions you approved, and only when you are signed in to your Wix dashboard. All traffic uses HTTPS. The diagnostics screen contains only counts and status codes, never customer information.

## 9. Children
The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.
